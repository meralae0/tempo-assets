# EN9 — "I Tested 5 FREE AI Video Tools With the Same Horror Scene" (no presenter, AI Studio voice)
Sections are separated by <long pause> in the TTS block; boundaries found with silencedetect.

S1 HOOK
One image. One prompt. Five free AI video tools. One of them asked me to pay before I could even start. One put me in a two hour queue. And one turned my shadow into a monster.

S2 THE TEST
Here's the test. I made this frame with Gemini: a girl in a yellow raincoat, a flickering bulb, and a shadow waiting at the end of the hallway. Then I gave every tool the exact same instruction: the bulb flickers, she slowly turns her head toward the door, and the shadow leans forward. Free accounts only, sign in with Google, default settings.

S3 VEO
First, Veo, inside Gemini. The first try failed: unusually high traffic, full capacity, try again later. The second try came back in about three minutes. Ten seconds, seven twenty p, no watermark, and the most cinematic result by far. But it went further than I asked: the shadow became a creature with glowing eyes. One catch: Veo is part of the Google AI Pro plan, so it's not free for everyone.

S4 PIXVERSE
Next, PixVerse. Sixty free credits, and one clip cost thirty eight. It was the fastest: under two minutes. Five seconds, five hundred and forty p, and a PixVerse watermark in the corner. And it didn't follow the prompt: instead of turning her head, she walks straight toward the door.

S5 HAILUO
Then Hailuo. Eighty free credits, and before uploading my image I had to confirm I own the rights to it. The default was two K and ultra wide, at sixty credits a clip. I switched to seven sixty eight p and sixteen by nine: thirty five credits. Two and a half minutes. It's the most faithful to my picture: the bulb flickers, she glances back, the shadow waits. But there's very little action, and a watermark too.

S6 KLING
Kling gave me sixty six free credits, and one five second clip at seven twenty p costs fifty. I pressed generate, and got this: you are in the queue, estimated wait longer than two hours. Forty five minutes later, it was still waiting. On the free plan, Kling is a lesson in patience.

S7 PIKA
And Pika? I signed in with Google and found zero credits. Nothing to test. Every list of free tools I read said Pika has free monthly credits. My account had none.

S8 SCOREBOARD
So here's the scoreboard. Best result: Veo, but it's in a paid plan. Most faithful free result: Hailuo. Fastest: PixVerse. Slowest: Kling. Not usable for free: Pika. And the real cost: one minute of video means about twelve clips. That's around four hundred and twenty credits on Hailuo, four hundred and fifty six on PixVerse, and six hundred on Kling. The free credits give you one or two clips. Five or ten seconds, not one minute.

S9 VERDICT
My advice: use the free credits to test your idea on one shot, pick the tool that follows your prompt, and only then pay for one month. The prompt, the image and the links are in the description. Some of them are affiliate links: if you sign up through them, it helps the channel, and it never changes the results you just saw.
